## Openings for Active AI
- **"You already own the sensor":** people with an Apple Watch, Fitbit, or Samsung watch already have sleep and resting-heart-rate data. Active AI turns it into a readiness score, free on every plan, with no ring. Frame it as *what Active AI does*, never as "same as Oura." Oura measures temperature, SpO₂, and more, and some features are FDA-regulated.
- **Readiness → action:** Oura tells you your readiness; Active AI's coach and workout plan act on it. A clear "score vs. score + plan" contrast.
- **Subscription fatigue:** "one app, one bill" (demo #48) lands with people tired of stacking ring + membership + separate apps.
- **Don't** claim anything about accuracy relative to Oura. There's no evidence to back it, and Active AI's score is a transparent heuristic, not a validated model (see `backend/lib/trends.ts`).
