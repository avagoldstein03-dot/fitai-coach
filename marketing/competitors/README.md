## Threat ranking
1. **Vora:** closest overlap (free tier, readiness, nutrition, cycle-aware, wearables). Small but moving fast.
2. **Fitbod:** the category leader for AI workouts; huge rating base. Workouts only.
3. **uFit AI:** same App Store subtitle as Fitbod, aggressive paywall. Competes on ASO and paid ads.
4. **Oura:** not an app competitor. It's the "readiness" reference point people already know, and the most useful foil.
5. **Trainera:** mostly B2B (trainer software). Low threat; worth watching for SEO.

## Biggest openings (details in each file)
- **vs. Fitbod:** Starter at $9.99 includes unlimited AI workouts **plus** nutrition and readiness, versus $15.99 for workouts only. Strong, checkable price and value claim.
- **vs. Oura:** "Already have an Apple Watch, Fitbit, or Samsung watch? You already have the data for a readiness score." No $349 ring. Don't claim feature parity; Oura measures much more.
- **vs. uFit:** a real free tier and an honest "estimate" label on the body scan (uFit's scan is its most-criticized feature).
- **vs. Vora:** life-stage (perimenopause/menopause) nutrition, injury-aware programming, ingredient safety, GLP-1 protein focus. Vora's cycle tracking overlaps the women's-health angle, so lean on *menopause/perimenopause*, not "cycle."
