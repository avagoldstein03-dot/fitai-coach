## Why this is the #1 threat
Vora overlaps the most with what Active AI leads with: a free tier, a readiness-style score, nutrition, a women's-health angle, and an AI coach. "Free readiness score" is **not unique** against Vora.

## Openings for Active AI
- **Life stage over cycle:** Vora does cycle tracking. Active AI's life-stage nutrition (perimenopause/menopause-aware protein targets) serves women *past* regular cycles, a segment cycle-tracking apps underserve. Lead with menopause/perimenopause, not "cycle."
- **Injury-aware programming + mobility substitution:** not claimed by Vora.
- **Ingredient safety check on scanned food:** not claimed by Vora.
- **GLP-1 protein tracking:** not claimed by Vora (organic content only, per ad rules).
- **Simplicity for non-lifters:** Vora's website pitch is "for lifters" and data-heavy (HRV, e1RM, per-muscle strain). Active AI's broad, any-age positioning and plain-English readiness explanation is the contrast. Don't copy their data-heavy messaging.
