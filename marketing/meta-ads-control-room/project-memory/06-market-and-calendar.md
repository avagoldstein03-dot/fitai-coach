# 06: Market & calendar

## Fitness seasonality (category pattern, not our data)

| Period | What happens | Implication |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Late Dec to Jan | New Year's resolutions: the biggest intent spike of the year. Auction costs also peak. | Have proven creative ready **before** Dec 26. Don't test new ideas in January; scale what's already proven. |
| Feb | Resolution drop-off | "Didn't stick last time?" angles (habit/consistency, readiness = permission to rest) |
| Late Mar to May | Pre-summer push | Body scan, protein, GLP-1 muscle-preservation angles (organic only for GLP-1, see 05) |
| Jun to Aug | Lower intent, cheaper auctions | Cheap testing window for new creative |
| Sep | Back-to-routine | Parent and genz (back to school) segments |
| Oct to Nov | Q4 retail competition raises costs, especially Black Friday / Cyber Monday week | Hold spend flat unless running an annual-plan promo |
