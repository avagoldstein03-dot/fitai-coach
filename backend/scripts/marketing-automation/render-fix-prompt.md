She is speaking directly to camera in a handheld phone selfie video, lip-syncing to the provided audio.

**Mouth movement — most important.** Her mouth moves the way a real person talks in casual conversation: small, relaxed, mostly horizontal. Do not exaggerate vowel shapes. Her mouth should never open into a wide round "O" or a large gaping shape — real conversational speech barely opens the jaw. Lips close fully on B, M and P sounds. Teeth show slightly on F and V. Between words the mouth returns to a soft, nearly-closed rest position, never held open. Subtle and understated throughout, as if she is talking to a friend rather than performing to camera.

**Capture format.** Shot at 30fps like a real iPhone, not 24fps — normal video motion cadence, not filmic. Genuine phone-camera characteristics: mild sensor noise, slight compression softness, imperfect autofocus that occasionally hunts, natural indoor white balance. Handheld throughout with the natural micro-shake and drift of a hand holding a phone. No zoom, no push-in, no reframing.

**Lighting.** Natural directional light from a single real source — a window or a room lamp — clearly coming from one side, not flat frontal illumination. Visible shadow shaping on the face: soft shadow under the nose, along one side of the jaw, and under the chin. Light falls off naturally across her face and into the background, brighter nearest the source, dimmer away from it. No ring light, no even frontal fill, no studio lighting, no shadowless illumination. Slight colour cast from the real light source rather than neutral white.

**This must look photographed, not rendered.** She is a real human being filmed on a phone, not a CGI character, not a 3D render, not a virtual influencer. Real skin with visible pores, slightly uneven tone, faint redness around the nose and under the eyes, a small blemish or two, natural oil sheen on the forehead and nose — not matte, not airbrushed, not smooth. Her face is asymmetric; features must not be perfectly mirrored. Individual flyaway hairs and an imperfect hairline. Absolutely no beauty filter, no skin smoothing, no glossy CGI surface, no doll-like or uncanny appearance.

**No phone screen in frame.** The phone she is holding must never show its screen to camera. Generated app interfaces come out as illegible garbled shapes. Either keep the phone out of frame entirely or show only its back or edge. The real app screenshot is cut in separately during editing.

**Phone scale.** If the phone is visible at all, it is a modern iPhone at true real-world size — roughly 6 inches tall, about the length of her palm plus fingers. Correct proportions relative to her hand and face; never oversized. Solid and rigid, holding exact shape for the whole clip — no warping, bending, shimmering or melting into her hand.

**Anatomy.** Correct, unambiguous human anatomy. Exactly two arms and two shoulders with a single clean shoulder line — no duplicated, extra, merged or floating limbs or shoulders anywhere in frame, including at the edges. Five fingers per hand with correct joint articulation. Her body holds the same shape throughout and does not shift or gain features between frames.

**Pacing.** She talks continuously with no dead air. No pause before she starts — she is already speaking at the first frame. Gaps between sentences stay under 0.4 seconds. The clip ends immediately on her final word with no trailing silence or held shot.

**Consistency.** She remains the same person throughout — face, hair and clothing identical from first frame to last. The background is a real room with genuine depth that holds its shape and does not morph, shift or flicker.
