# Active AI — Creator Guideline

Send this whole document to a creator. It's the only thing they get. There's no script,
and there won't be one — you know your audience and your format better than we do, and a
creator reading our words stops sounding like themselves.

---

## What Active AI is

A fitness app that covers the whole routine in one subscription instead of three:
workouts, nutrition logging, an AI coach you can actually ask questions, a daily
readiness score, and ingredient scanning.

The thing that makes it different from most of the category: **it doesn't need a
wearable.** It reads sleep and resting heart rate out of Apple Health — whatever's
already feeding it, including just a phone — so there's no ring or band to buy.

**Pricing:** free tier that's genuinely usable, then Starter $9.99 / Pro $15.99 /
Elite $19.99 per month. Annual works out cheaper per month. Billing runs through the
App Store or Play Store, so cancelling happens in phone settings.

## What actually sells — pick whatever fits you

You don't need to cover all of these. One or two, done in your own voice, beats a
feature list. These are ordered roughly by how well they land with people who've never
heard of us:

**The readiness score.** One number each morning for how hard to train today, with a
plain-language reason. Compares you to your own baseline, not an average person's. It's
free on every tier — including free — which surprises people.

**No wearable needed.** The direct comparison to a $300 ring or a $199/year band is the
single most concrete thing we have. Nobody expects the recovery number to be the free
part.

**One app instead of three.** Most people run a workout app, a food logger, and
something else. This is the consolidation argument, and it's the easiest one to make
honestly if it's true for you.

**The AI coach.** It knows what you logged — meals, workouts, goal — so answers pull
your real numbers rather than generic advice. Five messages a day free, unlimited paid.

**Ingredient scanning.** The same scan that logs your food also scores the additives.
Replaces needing a separate scanner app.

**Injury-aware workouts.** You enter an old injury at signup and the generated program
actually works around it — substitutes exercises, adds mobility work. Most apps collect
that field and ignore it.

**Weekly insights.** A written summary of your week in sentences rather than four
graphs to interpret yourself.

**Zero ads.** Anywhere, including on the free plan.

## Rules — these aren't negotiable

Everything above is optional. This part isn't.

1. **Actually use the app before you post about it.** This is what makes the whole thing
   legal. We can't use content from someone who hasn't.
2. **Disclose clearly and up front.** A visible #ad or the platform's paid-partnership
   label — not buried at the end of a hashtag block.
3. **Only your own real experience.** Don't describe results you didn't get.
4. **No outcome promises, especially with timelines.** No "lose 15lbs in 30 days." This
   gets ads rejected and generates one-star reviews from people it didn't work for.
5. **No medical claims.** The readiness score and body scan are not diagnostics. If you
   mention GLP-1s or peptides at all, any protein or nutrition number has to come from
   your doctor, not from us or the app.
6. **Don't name competitors negatively.** "A $300 recovery ring" is fine. Naming a brand
   and calling it bad is not.
7. **Don't claim accuracy we can't prove.** "It's matched how I felt" is your opinion
   and it's fine. "It's right every time" is a claim we'd have to substantiate.

## Things creators get wrong

- The readiness score is **free on every tier.** It's not a paid feature.
- There's **no wearable requirement.**
- Form Check and Precision Macros **are** paid (Pro and Elite).
- Nutrition data comes from Open Food Facts, which is crowd-sourced — very good coverage,
  occasional gaps. Don't say the database is complete.

## Format

Whatever works on your account. For reference, 15–45 seconds vertical tends to do the
work, and the first three seconds decide everything — but if your audience responds to
something else, do that instead.

**CTA:** your personal referral code. Anyone who uses it gets 7 days of Pro, and so do
you.
