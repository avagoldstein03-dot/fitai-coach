Privacy Policy

Active AI  ·  Last updated: October 2026

Active AI ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use Active AI:

  • Account information: name, email address, and authentication credentials (managed by Clerk).
  • Health & fitness data: age, sex, height, weight, fitness goals, activity level, dietary preferences, and food allergies.
  • Self-reported health context: injury history, life stage (such as perimenopause, if you choose to tell us), and medical conditions you select from a list during setup. Every one of these is optional, and "prefer not to say" is always an available answer. We use them only to make training and nutrition suggestions more cautious — never to diagnose, treat, or make decisions about you.
  • Apple Health data: if you connect Apple Health, we read steps, sleep duration, active energy, resting heart rate and weight, to calculate your daily readiness score. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and we never write anything back to Apple Health.
  • Body photos: photos you voluntarily submit for AI-powered body composition analysis.
  • Progress photos: photos you upload to track your fitness journey over time.
  • Food & nutrition data: meal photos, food diary entries, and calorie/macro information.
  • Workout data: exercise logs, session history, and workout programs.
  • Chat messages: messages you send to the AI coaching assistant.
  • Payment information: subscriptions are purchased and processed through the Apple App Store, and we receive only the subscription status, never your card details. If you are paid as an affiliate, payouts are handled by Stripe, who collect their own tax and banking details directly from you.
  • Device & usage data: device type, OS version, app usage events, and crash reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise your AI fitness coaching experience.
  • To analyse body composition, generate workout plans, and create nutrition recommendations.
  • To process subscription payments and manage your account.
  • To send push notifications relevant to your fitness goals (only if you opt in).
  • To improve the app through anonymised analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. AI Processing

Active AI uses third-party AI providers (OpenAI and Anthropic) to analyse photos and generate personalised recommendations. Photos and relevant profile data are transmitted to these providers solely for the purpose of generating your results. We do not permit these providers to use your data to train their models.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. Body scan and progress photos are stored in encrypted cloud storage (Amazon S3). We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following trusted service providers who help us operate the app:

  • Clerk – authentication
  • OpenAI / Anthropic – AI analysis
  • Stripe – payment processing
  • Amazon Web Services – file storage
  • PostHog – anonymised analytics
  • Firebase – push notifications

6. Health Data

The health and body composition data you provide is used exclusively to deliver your personalised coaching experience. We treat this data with the highest level of care and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Specifically, and without exception: data read from Apple Health is never used for advertising, never used in marketing, never sold, and never shared with any third party for their own purposes. It is used only to calculate your readiness score and to inform the coaching you asked for.

Active AI is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Nothing in the app should be used to decide whether to start, stop or change any medication or treatment — that is a conversation for your doctor.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at avagoldstein03@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Active AI is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
