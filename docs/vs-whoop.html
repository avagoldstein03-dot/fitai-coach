Active AI vs. Whoop

A phone-based coach vs. a wearable recovery tracker

Whoop is a wearable strap built specifically around recovery, sleep, and strain tracking, and it requires purchasing and wearing dedicated hardware. Active AI takes a different approach: it generates a daily Readiness Score directly from your phone, with no hardware purchase required, and layers full fitness and nutrition coaching on top of it.

Who Active AI fits better

If you want recovery guidance without buying a separate device, or you want fitness coaching, nutrition tracking, and body scan analysis all in one app rather than a single-purpose wearable, Active AI covers more ground from the phone you already carry.

Who Whoop fits better

If you specifically want continuous, sensor-based recovery and strain data throughout the day and night and are comfortable wearing a dedicated device for it, Whoop's hardware-based tracking measures things a phone alone can't.
