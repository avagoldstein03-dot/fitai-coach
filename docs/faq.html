Frequently Asked Questions

Active AI

What is Active AI?

Active AI is a mobile fitness and nutrition coaching app. It gives you a daily Readiness Score so you know whether to train hard or take it easy, an AI coach chat for fitness and nutrition questions, AI-powered body scan analysis, and nutrition tracking pulled from a real food database instead of crowd-sourced entries.

How does Active AI's Readiness Score work?

The Readiness Score looks at your recovery — synced from Apple Health or Samsung Health / Health Connect if you use a wearable, or self-reported if you don't — and tells you whether today is a day to push or a day to take it easy, instead of you having to guess.

Do I need a smartwatch or fitness tracker to use Active AI?

No. Active AI works from your phone without any wearable purchase required. If you already own a wearable or use Apple Health or Samsung Health, Active AI will sync with it for a more complete Readiness Score, but it's not required.

Does Active AI work with Apple Health and Samsung Health?

Yes. Active AI integrates with Apple HealthKit on iOS and with Samsung Health via Health Connect on Android.

Can Active AI help if I'm taking a GLP-1 medication?

Yes. Active AI sets higher protein targets for users on GLP-1 medications, since losing muscle along with fat is a common risk on these medications that isn't always explained upfront. The app also adjusts nutrition guidance for reduced appetite.

Does Active AI show ads?

No. Active AI has no ads. The app does not sell user data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How is Active AI's nutrition data different from apps like MyFitnessPal?

Active AI pulls ingredient and nutrition data from a real, verified food database rather than entries typed in by other users, which is the most common source of inaccurate calorie and macro counts in crowd-sourced nutrition apps. See the full Active AI vs. MyFitnessPal comparison.

Is Active AI only for people who are already fit?

No. Active AI is built for any age or fitness level, not a niche audience. It's designed to work for someone just starting out as well as someone experienced, and adjusts its guidance based on your actual profile rather than assuming one.

Does Active AI support people going through menopause?

Yes. Active AI adjusts nutrition guidance, including protein targets, for users who self-report menopause-related changes.

What is the Body Scan feature?

Body Scan uses AI to analyze a submitted photo or workout clip, giving feedback on body composition over time or on exercise form, such as squat depth or knee tracking.

How much does Active AI cost?

Active AI offers a free tier plus a paid subscription for full access to AI coaching, body scan, and nutrition tracking. Check the app for current pricing.

Is my health data private on Active AI?

Active AI does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with the trusted service providers needed to operate the app, such as authentication and payment processors, listed in full in the Privacy Policy.
